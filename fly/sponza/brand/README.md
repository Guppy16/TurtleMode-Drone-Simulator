# Brand assets

`cuds-badge.png` is the badge of the Cambridge University Drone Society (CUDS), shown on the
Sponza time trial's title and finish screens. It is used with the society's permission and is
not covered by this repository's MIT licence: do not reuse it outside this project without
asking the society.

The badge is the society's mark (the swoosh and the C, D roundels) cut from its full logo on a
transparent background, uncropped.
